# Full-life interview playbook

Use this playbook in create mode. The default is a **Full Personal OS** built from a person's life chronology and current reality. The interview is independent of why the user may later use AI.

Use the **Focused Context Pack** path only when the user explicitly requests a quick or single-domain result.

## Shared interview method

Discover meaning from evidence instead of asking the user to perform abstract self-analysis:

1. **Concrete scene:** What happened, when, and who was involved?
2. **Choice or response:** What did the user do?
3. **Reason and constraint:** What mattered or limited the options at the time?
4. **Outcome:** What happened next?
5. **Present meaning:** Does the user think it still affects current choices?
6. **Candidate interpretation:** Offer a narrow possible pattern for confirmation, revision, or rejection.

Facts and direct statements may become confirmed material. Meanings stated by the user are user interpretations. Patterns proposed by the agent remain working hypotheses until the user approves them.

## Turn design

- Ask one to three questions per turn and stay within one life period or domain.
- Prefer a concrete scene and one follow-up. Do not ask someone to summarize an entire childhood, career, or relationship history in one answer.
- Briefly reflect what was heard before moving on. Separate the event from any possible interpretation.
- Build a chronology first; derive working patterns only after examples from more than one period or an explicit confirmation.
- Collect only durable context that helps explain the person's life history, present reality, choices, or direction.
- When the user gives a short label such as “Web3,” ask what they actually did, how they entered it, what happened, and why it mattered.
- When the user says “I don't know,” narrow the time, offer two or three memory anchors, or ask about an observable scene. Do not record “no history” or move to a generic conclusion.

## Sensitive-domain transition

Childhood and family, important relationships, money/assets, and health can explain current constraints and recurring choices, but they are optional. Before each such module, say why it may be useful and make control explicit. For example:

> 接下来是可选的童年与家庭模块，因为早期生活有时会影响今天对安全、责任、工作和关系的选择。你可以跳过，只说大概，或告诉我这部分不要写进最终文件。

Do not use a single blanket consent at the beginning as permission for all sensitive topics. Let the user skip, generalize, answer without names, or exclude the material from files. Never presume trauma, abuse, conflict, diagnosis, financial distress, or relationship problems.

## Path A — Full Personal OS (default)

The sequence is chronological but adaptive. A serious full interview will usually take more than ten short rounds and may span sessions. Merge a phase only when the user's history makes it genuinely unnecessary. A skipped sensitive phase remains visibly “skipped” or “not assessed,” never “irrelevant.”

### 1. Opening and life coordinates

Collect preferred name, language, approximate age or life stage, where the user grew up, and their current broad living situation. Do not ask why they want a Personal OS, why they use AI, or what task they want AI to perform. No AI knowledge, terminology, or preparation is needed.

A good first round:

1. “我应该怎么称呼你？我们主要用什么语言聊？”
2. “你大概是哪一年出生的，主要在哪里长大？”
3. “你现在生活在哪座城市，当前主要处于上学、工作、待业、创业、照顾家庭，还是几件事同时发生？”

### 2. Childhood and family of origin — optional

The aim is not therapy or a trauma inventory. Establish the environment, early responsibilities, repeated messages, discontinuities, and any concrete event the user believes still matters.

Ask across several turns as needed:

- “你主要在哪里、和谁长大？那段生活总体比较稳定，还是经常搬家、换照顾者或变化很大？”
- “小时候家里最常强调什么——例如成绩、懂事、赚钱、安全、独立或照顾别人？能想到一个具体场景吗？”
- “你什么时候第一次觉得自己需要承担超出同龄人的责任？当时发生了什么？”
- “有没有一件早期经历，后来明显影响了你选择学校、工作、城市、关系或金钱的方式？”

Do not insist on all prompts. Follow the chronology and the user's willingness.

### 3. Education and early independence

Build the path through school, training, first independent choices, and important changes in environment. Ask why a path was chosen, what reality differed from expectation, and what the user learned about themselves through action.

- “中学之后，你做过的第一个真正由自己决定的选择是什么？为什么这么选？”
- “学校、专业或培训是怎么选的？当时有哪些别的选择？”
- “哪一段学习经历让你第一次发现自己擅长或不适合什么？发生过什么具体事情？”
- “有没有退学、转专业、考试、搬家、出国或其他转折？它改变了后面哪一步？”

### 4. Work, projects, and career chronology

Walk through meaningful roles and projects in order. This is not merely resume collection. For each important transition, establish entry, actual responsibility, people served, outcome, reason for staying or leaving, and what changed afterward.

Start concretely:

- “第一份对你真正重要的工作或项目是什么？你怎么进去的？”
- “你实际负责什么，而不是职位名称写了什么？”
- “那段经历里最值得讲的一件事是什么？你做了什么，结果怎样？”
- “为什么离开、结束或转向下一段？”

Repeat the same transition logic for later stages. If the user has entrepreneurship, independent projects, creative work, caregiving, informal labor, or a career gap, treat it as real history rather than forcing a conventional employment timeline.

### 5. Important people and relationships — optional

This can include partner, family, mentors, friends, collaborators, or a past relationship. Seek only decision-relevant effects on trust, boundaries, responsibility, place, time, or risk. Do not solicit intimate detail.

- “有没有一个人或一段关系，明显改变了你对信任、边界或承诺的理解？发生了什么？”
- “你现在承担哪些家庭或关系角色？它们实际怎样影响城市、时间、收入或风险选择？”
- “在合作或亲密关系里，哪种反复出现的情况最容易让你舒服或消耗？”

Route useful detail to `references/RELATIONSHIPS.md` only when the user explicitly wants it retained. The core should contain at most the minimum durable consequence.

### 6. Money, assets, and risk — optional

Understand how financial responsibilities and ownership affect choices, not account balances for their own sake. Never collect credentials, full account numbers, private keys, recovery material, or unnecessary exact identifiers.

- “你现在做职业或生活决定时，主要要保护哪些财务责任或安全底线？”
- “过去有没有一次赚钱、亏损、负债、投资或家庭责任，改变了你后来承担风险的方式？”
- “哪些资源是你可以投入的，哪些资产或责任不能轻易动？”

Exact amounts are optional. Route detailed retained information to `references/ASSETS.md` only with explicit approval and clear sensitivity rules.

### 7. Health, energy, and daily-life constraints — optional

Ask about practical effects rather than diagnoses. Medical advice is outside the profile's authority.

- “有没有睡眠、精力、身体状况或照护责任，会真实限制你的工作时间或选择？”
- “什么样的节奏让你长期状态更好？最近一次状态明显变好或变差是什么时候？”
- “这些状态通常怎样影响你的日常安排或重要选择？你愿意让最终档案保留到什么程度？”

### 8. Present responsibilities and active life structure

Map what currently competes for time, attention, capital, and accountability. Separate durable responsibilities from temporary project status.

- “现在有哪些人、工作或事情会固定占用你的时间和责任？”
- “最近一次两个重要领域发生冲突是什么？你最后牺牲了什么？”
- “哪些现状可能很快变化，不应该写成长期身份？”

### 9. Direction and desired life

Ground direction in observable horizons and tradeoffs, not slogans.

- “如果未来一年进展顺利，工作和日常生活会具体有什么不同？”
- “把时间拉到三到五年，你希望自己拥有或能够选择什么？”
- “哪种看起来很成功的人生，其实不是你想要的？”
- “为了这个方向，你愿意暂时承受什么？什么代价不值得？”

### 10. Repeated choices and working patterns

Now compare evidence from different life periods. Ask for a counterexample before turning a pattern into a working hypothesis.

- “前面几段经历里，你似乎在___时会选择___。这符合你的感觉吗？有没有一次相反的例子？”
- “遇到不确定问题时，你最近一次是怎么开始的？”
- “讲一次你坚持太久，以及一次你及时停止的经历。两次判断依据有什么不同？”
- “什么时候速度比完整更重要？什么时候不能先凑合？”

### 11. Reconciliation, privacy, and scope

Review contradictions neutrally. Ask which context controls rather than resolving it for the user. Confirm:

- facts that may be outdated or need evidence;
- domains intentionally skipped or not assessed;
- details allowed in the compact core, private routed references, or nowhere;
- live project state that belongs outside the Personal OS;
- candidate working hypotheses the user accepts, revises, or rejects.

Only then move to the fact audit.

## Post-audit agent configuration — separate from the life interview

Only after the user has reviewed the personal fact audit, ask a short separate set of operating questions about how an agent may use the approved context. This is configuration, not biography, and must not change the facts, emphasis, or structure of the completed life interview.

- “如果你的想法有漏洞，你希望 AI 怎样指出？什么语气或做法会让你反感？”
- “哪些只读分析和草稿可以直接做？哪些写入、花钱、发布、联系别人或不可逆操作必须先问你？”
- “哪些个人信息只能留在私有参考文件，不能出现在对外材料里？”

Do not ask what the user plans to use AI for. Configure general collaboration and approval boundaries that remain useful across tools and tasks.

## Path B — Focused Context Pack (explicit only)

When the user explicitly asks for a quick or single-domain pack:

1. Name the narrow scope and say it will not represent the whole person.
2. Interview the relevant chronology, concrete work, constraints, patterns, privacy, and AI boundaries.
3. Ask only about other life domains that materially constrain the chosen domain, with the same sensitive-topic controls.
4. Label unassessed domains and do not infer them.
5. Deliver a clearly named focused context file or folder; never call it a complete Personal OS.

A focused career pack may be useful for immediate job-search work. It is not a shortcut to Full Personal OS depth.

## Questions to avoid

Do not lead with prompts such as:

- “哪些经历塑造了你？”
- “你的优势和弱点是什么？”
- “你的核心价值观是什么？”
- “你如何做决定？”
- “你最重要的人生角色是什么？”
- “描述一下你的性格。”

These may appear only as late confirmation after concrete history exists. The agent is responsible for making questions answerable.

## Readiness for the fact audit

For a **Full Personal OS**, do not begin the audit until:

- the major life stages have a usable chronology;
- baseline work, present responsibilities, direction, and repeated choices have evidence;
- each sensitive module is either covered at the user's chosen depth or explicitly marked skipped/not assessed;
- proposed patterns are supported by more than one example, a counterexample check, or direct user confirmation;
- temporary concerns and changing project state are separated from durable identity.

For a **Focused Context Pack**, audit only the declared scope and list the unassessed domains.

Every proposed statement must be an explicit user statement, a concrete example with user-confirmed meaning, or a clearly labeled unresolved/working hypothesis. If the result would be generic enough to describe many people, continue interviewing or deliver a smaller honest scope rather than padding it.
